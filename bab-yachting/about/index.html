About BAB Yachting

Engineering with intent.

A Türkiye-based studio connecting naval design, structural development and bespoke yacht exterior equipment.

01 — Our approach

Closer to the idea.
Closer to the build.

We believe the strongest marine solutions emerge when design and engineering stay in conversation. That is why we look at the whole vessel while resolving the smallest mechanism.

The supplied work spans exterior concepts, SAFINA 92 FT general arrangement, complete hull structures and moving equipment assemblies. That range reflects BAB Yachting's position between design intent and manufacturing reality in Türkiye's active yacht-building landscape.
